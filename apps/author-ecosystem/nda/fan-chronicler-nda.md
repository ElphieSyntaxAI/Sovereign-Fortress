# Fan & Chronicler Agreement (Lore-Gate)

**Parties:** The **Author** (rights holder) and **you** (the **Fan** or **Chronicler**). **ElphieSyntax** operates **Lore-Gate** access controls as technical infrastructure.

**Purpose:** **Gamified legal protection** for the community: clear rules for gated lore, anti-scraping, contribution licensing, and sanctions for leaks.

---

## 1. Gated access (personal use only)

Fans agree that **Wiki Drafts**, **Unlocked Lore**, **Gated Lore**, and other Lore-Gate materials are licensed **solely for personal enjoyment** and **private, non-commercial** reading inside the platform. **No** redistribution, resale, mirroring, or “share packs” of gated text.

---

## 2. Anti-scraping & Fan-Fic AI

**Automated harvesting** of the Wiki or Lore-Gate endpoints—whether via bots, headless browsers, bulk export scripts, or API abuse—to build datasets for **“Fan-Fic AI,”** fan-model training, or **unauthorized derivative databases** is a **material breach**. Human, manual note-taking for personal use is not automated harvesting; **systematic** scraping is.

---

## 3. Community contributions (canon license)

Any **Fan Theories**, **Lore Suggestions**, **Chronicler notes**, or similar submissions transmitted to the Author **through the platform** grant the Author a **royalty-free, perpetual, worldwide license** to **incorporate, adapt, attribute, or discard** those ideas in the **official canon**, subject to moderation and the Author’s editorial discretion. You represent you have rights to grant the license.

---

## 4. Sanctions (spoilers & logic hooks)

**Leaking** unreleased **Logic Hooks**, **outline beats**, **Vault spoilers**, or other embargoed narrative mechanics—whether on-platform or off—may result in **immediate** **Lore-Gate** revocation, loss of **Chronicler** status, account suspension, and any other remedies under community rules or applicable law.

---

## 5. Term

Obligations under §§2–4 survive **two (2) years** after Lore-Gate access ends or is revoked, whichever is later, unless channel rules specify otherwise.

---

## 6. Severability

If any restriction is void in your jurisdiction, it is severed; the remainder remains enforceable to the maximum extent.
