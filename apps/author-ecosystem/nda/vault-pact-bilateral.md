## 1. Vault Seal — Narrative DNA & Architect obligations (ElphieSyntax ↔ Author)

### 1.1 Confidentiality of Narrative Data

ElphieSyntax acknowledges that all data stored in **`public.p4_manuscripts`** (including, without limitation, manuscript body, outline, revision metadata, and tenant-scoped narrative columns) and **`public.p4_hal_ledger`** (including `keystroke_latency_ms`, `stylometric_snapshot`, `raw_sample`, session identifiers, and related JSONB) constitutes the Sovereign’s **“Narrative DNA”**: the fused record of **creative expression** and **human-authenticity telemetry** used to evidence authorship. Narrative DNA is **confidential** to the Sovereign except as expressly processed under this instrument and the published Technical Annex.

### 1.2 The Architect’s Promise (human access boundary)

ElphieSyntax **warrants** that it shall **not** access, read, or perform **discretionary human analysis** on the Sovereign’s Narrative DNA **except** where:

- processing is **automated** and **triggered by the Sovereign** (e.g., narrative logic / sensitivity auditing, ingestion, vectorization, HAL session commits, Lore-Git workflows, or exports the Sovereign initiates);  
- access is required for **security, integrity, or lawful process** (e.g., abuse investigation, subpoena, cryptographic verification) under least-privilege controls; or  
- the Sovereign grants **separate written** authorization for a named support or legal workflow.

Routine “browsing” of manuscript prose by Architect personnel for curiosity, QA, or model curation is **prohibited** unless covered above.

### 1.3 Zero-Training Warranty (Librarian & Critic)

Content processed through **Librarian** (Google **Gemini**) or **Critic** (Anthropic **Claude**) is transmitted only via **Enterprise API** tiers (or successor enterprise offerings) configured, to the extent commercially available, to **exclude** customer prompts/completions from vendors’ **global foundation-model training** pools. ElphieSyntax **warrants** that it contracts for such exclusions where available and does **not** use Sovereign Vault text to train its **own** general-purpose weights. Vendor DPAs and product settings control where they exceed Architect commitments.

### 1.4 Stateless bicameral inference (technical elaboration)

Dual-provider auditing operates **statelessly** at the application layer: prompts and completions are not written into Architect **training corpora**; ephemeral vendor logs may exist under vendor policies. Transport uses **TLS 1.2+**. Neither pass may be covertly routed to consumer-grade endpoints where enterprise **data exclusion** flags are available and economically feasible.

### 1.5 Aggregates only

Fleet-wide metrics must be **k-anonymized** and **non-reversible** relative to any single Sovereign manuscript. Any statistic that could enable reconstruction of Narrative DNA is **prohibited**.

## 2. Keystroke latency telemetry (HAL) — non-reconstructive

**2.1 Definition.** “**Keystroke Latency Telemetry**” means **inter-event timing** and **rhythm statistics** (e.g., flight times between eligible key events, IME composition block latencies) for **HAL** scoring. It is **not** a lossless Unicode key log sufficient alone to reconstruct the manuscript.

**2.2 Persistence.** HAL rows in **`p4_hal_ledger`** store timing arrays and JSON digests (`stylometric_snapshot`, `raw_sample`) as **evidentiary summaries**, including optional bounded **keystroke DNA** only under separate consent.
