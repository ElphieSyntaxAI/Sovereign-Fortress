# Publisher & Legal Entity Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Focus:** Human Authorship Reports, ledger exports, and pre-publication materials.

---

## 1. Confidential information

Confidential Information includes **Human Authorship Reports**, `p4_hal_ledger`-derived exports, stylometric snapshots, revision-gate metadata, and any unreleased manuscript or bible materials the Author shares under NDA.

---

## 2. Obligations

You will use Confidential Information **only** for diligence, registration, or defense of the Author’s work as authorized. You will limit internal distribution to personnel with a **need to know** and will not use exports to train public models or competitive databases.

---

## 3. Press / marketing

No public statement implying endorsement of the System’s forensic conclusions without **mutual** written approval.

---

## 4. Term

**Five (5) years** from receipt of each Confidential Information bundle, unless a master publishing NDA states otherwise.

---

## 5. Regulatory production

If compelled to produce materials by lawful process, you will notify the Author **promptly** where legally permitted and seek protective treatment of sensitive telemetry.
