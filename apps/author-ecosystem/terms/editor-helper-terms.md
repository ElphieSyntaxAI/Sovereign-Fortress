# Editor & Helper Terms (Collaborators)

**Effective focus:** Confidentiality, work-for-hire status, and non-interference with the author’s HAL score.

---

## 1. Confidentiality and the Vault

You may receive access to the Author’s **Vault** materials (including RAG-retrieved bible, wiki drafts where authorized, outlines, and related project context).

You agree **not** to disclose, scrape, republish, train models on, or use Vault materials for **any purpose outside** the specific project engagement authorized by the Author or their legal representative.

---

## 2. Role limitation and IP

Your contributions (including comments, suggested edits, structural notes, or logic overrides facilitated by the System) may be recorded and tagged as **Collaborator Input** in ledgers, audit trails, or revision metadata.

To the maximum extent permitted by law and except where a separate written agreement states otherwise, you **waive moral rights** and **assign or waive intellectual property claims** in favor of the **Author** for deliverables integrated into the Author’s final work product as directed by the Author.

If you require a different IP arrangement, you must execute a **separate written agreement** before relying on these terms.

---

## 3. HAL integrity

You agree **not** to paste or inject **external third-party AI-generated text** into the manuscript in a manner intended to circumvent authenticity tracking.

Edits that materially affect the manuscript should be performed within the **Authenticity Environment** (integrated editors, approved clients, or workflows designated by the platform) so the Author’s **HAL score** and related forensic signals remain meaningful.

---

## 4. General

Breach of confidentiality or HAL integrity rules may result in **access revocation**, **project removal**, and other remedies described in the main Terms of Service or separate engagement contract.
