## 2. HAL ledger consent (keystroke dynamics)

You **explicitly consent** to the collection and processing of **keystroke dynamics**—meaning **timing and rhythm telemetry** between key events (for example, inter-key intervals and related flight-time statistics)—for the purpose of computing signals such as a **Human Authenticity Quotient** (or equivalent HAL score) and related forensic baselines.

This telemetry is stored in the **`p4_hal_ledger`** (and related authorized stores) together with derived summaries (for example, stylometric snapshots and session metadata) and may be used as **evidentiary support** of human authorship **subject to your agreement, applicable law, and technical limitations of the service**.

You understand that keystroke dynamics are **behavioral measurements** and may be treated as sensitive in some jurisdictions; you should review the main Privacy Policy and jurisdictional addenda.

If a provider’s terms, product tier, or region-specific settings differ, the **controlling vendor documentation** and your **Data Processing Addendum** prevail for that subprocessed processing.
