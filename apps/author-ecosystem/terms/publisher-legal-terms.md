# Publisher & Legal Entity Terms

**Effective focus:** Audit access, compliance reports, and export protocols.

---

## 1. Verification access (Human Authorship Report)

Upon **explicit Author approval** (and subject to technical availability), the System may generate a **Human Authorship Report** derived from signals stored in the **`p4_hal_ledger`** and related forensic artifacts.

This report is intended to support **publisher due diligence** and **copyright registration workflows** but is **not** a government certification and **does not guarantee** registration or litigation outcomes.

---

## 2. Immutable export and digital seal

Exports prepared for publication may include a **Digital Seal** or bundle metadata referencing **revision gates**, **stylometric snapshots**, and ledger pointers.

“Immutable export” means exports are designed to be **tamper-evident or integrity-linked** to platform records at export time; downstream systems must preserve that integrity if you rely on it in disputes.

---

## 3. Liability and final vetting

The System may provide **Narrative Auditing** (including logic and sensitivity critiques via automated models). **Publishers remain solely responsible** for final legal vetting before mass distribution, including without limitation **libel**, **privacy**, **right of publicity**, **trademark**, **comparative advertising**, and **territorial compliance**.

Automated audits are **assistive** and may produce **false positives or false negatives**.

---

## 4. Data processing

Where Publishers receive exports containing personal or behavioral data about authors, Publishers agree to process such data in accordance with applicable law, the Author’s instructions, and any executed **Data Processing Addendum**.

---

## 5. General

These Publisher terms supplement—not replace—your negotiated publishing agreement with the Author.
